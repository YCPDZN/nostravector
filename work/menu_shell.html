GUIDE

Six endings await at Carax. Your earlier choices and the companion actively following you determine the route. Check your party before accepting the final Sagittarius A* warning.

Good and Neutral unlock Chapter 5, sandbox exploration and travel toward Andromeda. All four bad endings end the game and erase this character’s save. Use SAVE AS beforehand if you want a separate backup.

GOOD / Save the world
  1. Recruit Orion Mascar in Chapter 2, before first leaving Earth.
  2. Recruit Nyrexia Erisconos before the Chapter 3 coup and exile from Sol.
  3. Spare James Brown.
  4. Spare Malia de Toro after the Janira Prime battle.
  5. Spare Croxian Mascar.
  6. Complete the story and defeat Lord Zark on Carax.

All five decisions are required. Recruiting Orion or Nyrexia later does not replace their early recruitment.

NEUTRAL / Try your best
  1. Miss at least one Good-ending requirement, while avoiding the full bad-choice combination below.
  2. For a reliable example: recruit Orion in Chapter 2, miss Nyrexia before the coup, but spare James, Malia and Croxian.
  3. Complete the story and defeat Lord Zark on Carax.

A mixture of merciful and missed or hostile decisions leads here. Killing Orion alone does not force a bad ending; the full bad-choice combination is also required.

BAD 1 / Orion Mascar
  1. Recruit Orion so his revenge story can unfold.
  2. Do not recruit Nyrexia before the coup.
  3. Kill James Brown, Malia de Toro and Croxian Mascar.
  4. Leave the hostile, vengeful Orion alive. Do not finish defeating him.
  5. Ignore the warning about Orion and finish the Carax battle.

Orion returns for revenge. A following companion does not override this ending while the vengeful Orion remains alive.

BAD 2 / Lord Zark
  1. Do not recruit Nyrexia before the coup, and kill James Brown, Malia and Croxian.
  2. Finish defeating Orion Mascar before committing to the final battle. This also changes the snarky warning if you had already seen it.
  3. Make sure neither Josiah Richards nor Tyrotaronus is actively following you.
  4. Finish the Carax battle.

Lord Zark delivers the fatal ending. Keep those two companions aboard your ship or assigned as governors to avoid their betrayal routes.

BAD 3 / Josiah Richards — The Prime Secretary
  1. Do not recruit Nyrexia before the coup, and kill James Brown, Malia and Croxian.
  2. Finish defeating Orion Mascar.
  3. Have the Project BC imported Josiah Richards actively following you, rather than stationed on the ship, governing a world or serving another faction.
  4. Accept the warning: “Be careful around the ones you trust.” Then finish the Carax battle.

Josiah’s betrayal takes priority if both he and Tyrotaronus are following you.

BAD 4 / Tyrotaronus
  1. Do not recruit Nyrexia before the coup, and kill James Brown, Malia and Croxian.
  2. Finish defeating Orion Mascar.
  3. Recruit Tyrotaronus on Tamaz and have him actively following you. He must not be stationed on the ship, governing a world or serving another faction.
  4. Make sure Josiah Richards is not actively following you.
  5. Accept the warning: “Be careful around the ones you trust.” Then finish the Carax battle.

Tyrotaronus reveals the Oilkoumeneus and turns against you.
