SUIT WARNING
WEATHER WARNING! SUPER BLIZZARD INCOMING!

NARRATOR — HAZARD TUTORIAL

1. WEATHER CAN KILL
Your suit has detected a super blizzard. Visibility will fall, and staying outside will drain your suit’s heat.

2. WATCH YOUR SUIT HEAT
Watch your suit heat gauge. The station and the rover protect you from the cold. At zero heat, your suit performs an emergency recovery at the station.

3. SAVE YOUR STAMINA
Sprinting drains stamina. Use it for short escapes, then walk to recover. Never exhaust yourself far from shelter.

4. FIND YOUR WAY IN A WHITEOUT
When the whiteout hides your path, follow the station beacon or open your field map with M. Landmarks may disappear; the beacon keeps pointing home.

5. SHELTER IS SURVIVAL
Get inside the station and let your heat recover. Stay with James while the storm passes. This super blizzard lasts five minutes of active game time.
